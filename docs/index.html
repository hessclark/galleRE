galleRE

Organize, reorder, and resize real-estate photos for MLS upload — fast.

🍎 macOS only · free & open source
⬇ Download for Mac View on GitHub

Universal build — Apple Silicon & Intel · macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later

What it does

🖱️

Drag to reorder

Arrange photos in the exact order you want them on the MLS. A number badge shows each photo's position.

🔢

Save the order

Renames files so Finder — and the MLS uploader — keep your order every time.

🗜️

Resize for MLS

Batch-resize to the perfect upload size with one click. Your originals are never touched.

🔄

Convert formats

HEIC, PNG, TIFF and even PDF files convert straight to MLS-ready JPG on export.

👁️

Quick preview

Tap a photo and hit Space for a big preview; arrow keys flip through the set.

⚙️

Your settings

Choose naming style, resize target, quality and output folder in a simple export wizard.

Install on your Mac

  1. Download galleRE-macOS.zip and unzip it.
  2. Drag galleRE.app into your Applications folder.
  3. First launch: right-click (or Control-click) the app → Open → Open again.
Why the right-click? galleRE is open source and ad-hoc signed (not paid Apple notarization), so the first time you open it macOS asks you to confirm. Right-click → Open tells your Mac you trust it — you only do this once. (Or: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.)
